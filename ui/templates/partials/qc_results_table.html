What do these verdicts mean?
Adequate
OLG provides clear, actionable guidance for this scenario. The error happened despite the policy being documented.
Partial
OLG addresses the topic but is missing something material — typically a specific threshold, exception, BEACON procedure, or guidance on how two policies interact. Partial does not mean the OLG is silent; it usually means a worker would need to synthesize across multiple pages to get the full answer.
Gap
OLG does not address this policy or procedure at all. A worker would have no written guidance to consult.
Error
The judge API call failed before producing a verdict (e.g., out of credit, rate limit, network). Not a real assessment — re-run investigate to replace.
